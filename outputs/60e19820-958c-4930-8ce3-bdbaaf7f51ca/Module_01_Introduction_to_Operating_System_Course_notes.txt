# Introduction to Operating System Course

**Overview**: This course introduces the design and underlying principles of operating systems, covering both theoretical concepts and practical implementation through lab exercises. The course aims to provide a deeper understanding of how operating systems work and how they support applications. By studying this course, students will gain a better understanding of the basics of current systems and how to develop portable applications.

**Key Takeaways**:
* The course covers the design and underlying principles of operating systems, including theoretical concepts and practical implementation.
* Understanding operating systems is essential to develop applications that can run on different operating systems and to ensure portability.
* The course includes lab exercises to provide hands-on experience and a better understanding of the concepts discussed in theory.

**Important Terms or Definitions**: None explicitly defined in this segment, but key concepts include operating system design, underlying principles, and practical implementation.
