# Introduction to Operating System Course

**Overview**: This course introduces the fundamentals of operating systems, focusing on their design and underlying principles. The course will cover both theoretical concepts and practical implementation, providing a comprehensive understanding of operating systems. Through lectures and lab exercises, students will gain hands-on experience with operating system mechanisms.

**Key Takeaways**:
* The course will study the design of operating systems and their underlying concepts.
* Practical implementation of operating system concepts and mechanisms will be explored.
* Lab exercises will be used to help students understand and apply operating system principles.

**Important Terms**: Operating System (OS) - a software that manages computer hardware and provides a platform for running applications.
