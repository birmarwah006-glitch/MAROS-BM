# Introduction to Operating System Course

**Overview**: This course introduces the design and underlying principles of operating systems, covering both theoretical concepts and practical implementation through lab exercises. The course aims to provide a deeper understanding of how operating systems work and how they support applications. By studying this course, students will gain a better understanding of the basics of operating systems and how they facilitate user programs and applications.

**Key Takeaways**:
* The course covers the design and underlying principles of operating systems, including theoretical concepts and practical implementation.
* Understanding operating systems is essential for developing applications that run on different operating systems and ensuring their portability.
* The course includes a lab component to provide hands-on experience with implementing operating system concepts.
* Studying operating systems helps students understand the basics of how current systems are developed and how they support applications.
* The course will explore how operating systems facilitate user programs and applications, including the support and treatment they provide.

**Important Terms**: Operating System (OS), Portability, Lab Exercises.
